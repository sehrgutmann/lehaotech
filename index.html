Why SKIELD

Built for the Outdoors

Every pole is engineered for ease of assembly, long-term durability, and versatile field performance.

Easy Plug-In Assembly

Push-pin connection system lets you assemble or disassemble poles in seconds — no tools required. Ideal for quick campsite setup.

Adjustable Height

Telescoping sections allow flexible height configuration to suit tarps, awnings, canopies and tent shelters of various sizes.

Durable Aluminum Alloy

High-grade aluminum alloy body with metal cover cap ensures long-lasting performance in harsh outdoor environments.

Anti-Slip Knurled Tip

Specially knurled metal ground tip provides maximum grip on soil, grass, and gravel — prevents poles from sinking or slipping.

Complete Kit Included

Each set comes with 5m rope per pole and a convenient carry bag — everything you need right out of the box.

Export-Ready Supply

Produced and supplied by Jiangsu Inter-China Group — experienced B2B outdoor hardware exporter serving global markets.
