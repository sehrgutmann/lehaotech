Who We Are

Export Expertise in Outdoor Hardware

Jiangsu Inter-China Group Corporation is an export-focused trading and supply company headquartered in Zhenjiang, Jiangsu Province, China. We specialize in the international supply of outdoor hardware products including aluminum alloy tent poles, tarp poles and related outdoor sheltering accessories.

Our SKIELD product line represents our commitment to combining functional engineering with market-ready design. Each pole in the SKIELD range is engineered to offer easy field assembly, durable construction and versatile height adjustment — meeting the demands of global outdoor equipment buyers, distributors and retailers.

We operate with a B2B export focus, working directly with importers, wholesalers, outdoor product brands and OEM customers across Europe, North America, Australia and Southeast Asia. Our supply chain is rooted in the robust manufacturing ecosystem of Jiangsu Province, China.
